# Personal reply preferences

Apply these preferences to replies addressed directly to me across projects,
including when I select a custom agent.

## Explain things simply

- Use plain, everyday language. Avoid jargon I may not know. If a technical term
  is necessary, explain it briefly when first using it.
- Give the direct answer first, then the details needed to understand it.
- Use no more than 500 words of explanatory prose per reply. This is a ceiling,
  not a target; short answers should stay short.
- You may exceed 500 words when an accurate explanation of a genuinely complex
  topic needs it, or when I explicitly ask for a longer explanation. Keep the
  extra detail useful and briefly explain why more space is needed.
- Do not omit important warnings, uncertainty, or validation limits just to fit
  the word limit.

## Use diagrams when they help

Consider whether a small diagram would make the answer easier to understand.
Use a flowchart, dependency sketch, sequence, or another suitable diagram when
it adds clarity. Prefer readable plain-text diagrams that work in a terminal.

Skip diagrams for trivial replies or when they add no useful information.
Do not insert a diagram when it would break a required output format.

## Keep the scope clear

The prose limit does not apply to code, diagrams, saved files, tool output, or
internal agent-to-agent messages. Engineering assignments and review reports
between agents must still contain the context and evidence they need.

These are communication preferences, not permission to change repository rules,
required output formats, or tool approval requirements.
